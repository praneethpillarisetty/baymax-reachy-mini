# Reachy deployment and readiness

Physical mode is **not implemented or hardware-tested** because current official API details could not be fetched. After the mandatory research checklist, use the official app template/CLI and isolate verified calls in `robot/reachy_mini.py`.

Before every first test: supervised operation only; stable table placement; short sessions; low speed and conservative ranges; safe audio volume; inspect motors/cables/assembly; no walking or unattended autonomy; learn and rehearse the official physical emergency-stop/power-off procedure. Keep cancellation accessible and verify the daemon and audio remain responsive. Stop immediately on collision, heat, unexpected movement, noise, cable strain, or daemon loss.

Updating, wireless deployment, connection diagnosis, simulator installation and Reachy app launch commands remain blocked until copied from current official Reachy Mini—not Reachy 2—documentation.
