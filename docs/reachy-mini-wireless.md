# Reachy Mini Wireless

Wireless uses its onboard CM4. `reachy-mini.local` or a reachable address is only a candidate and never proof of robot identity. Verify the official daemon/SDK network connection. Recommended deployment is `wireless-hybrid`: local robot audio only after validation and laptop Ollama over an explicitly enabled private LAN. Do not put qwen3:4b or laptop faster-whisper-small on CM4. Tiny STT and ARM64 Piper remain unsupported until physical benchmarks confirm runtime, storage, thermals, and audio.
